APP NAME
Finora: Finance Tracker

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Track spending, manage budgets, and make progress toward savings goals.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Make everyday money tracking easier with Finora. Record your income and expenses, organize purchases, and see how your spending adds up.

With Finora, you can:
- Record income and expenses with categories, dates, and notes.
- Search and review your transaction history.
- Set category budgets and track spending against each limit.
- Create savings goals and follow your progress.
- View summaries of your balance, income, and expenses.
- Ask the in-app money coach about your recorded finances. Online AI responses require an internet connection and an available service.
- Explore as a guest, or sign in to save and sync your records.

Finora uses US dollars (USD). Guest data is temporary and may be lost when you leave or reset the app. Signed-in records can be managed from your account. Finora is a personal tracking tool, not financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Money coach responses may be inaccurate; check important figures and decisions.
