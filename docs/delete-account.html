Delete your Finora account

This page lets Finora: Finance Tracker users request account deletion even if the app is not installed.

Request deletion by email

Email remphearomthunmony@gmail.com with the subject “Finora account deletion” and include the email address associated with your Finora account. Do not send your password or PIN. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

What is deleted

After verifying the request, we delete the Firebase Authentication account and its Finora profile, transactions, budgets, and savings goals. You can also delete your account directly in the app from Profile > Delete account. That flow may ask you to sign in again first.

Guest data is temporary and is not associated with an account. The app does not sync guest records to Firebase.

See the Privacy Policy for details about data handling and deletion.
