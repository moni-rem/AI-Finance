Privacy Policy

Finora: Finance Tracker
Effective date: 27 October 2026

Finora (“we”, “us”, or “the app”) helps you track personal finances. This policy describes information handled by the current app.

Information you provide

Firebase Authentication processes your email and sign-in credentials. Google sign-in also provides account identity details. For signed-in users, Finora stores a display name and email, transactions (including names, categories, amounts, dates, and optional notes), budgets, and savings goals in Firebase Cloud Firestore. Optional phone and location profile fields are currently held in app memory and are not synced to Firestore. A security PIN is stored on your device using secure storage.

Guest use

Guest activity is temporary, kept in app memory, and is not synced to a Firebase account. It may be cleared when you leave or reset the app. Online AI requests require signing in.

AI assistant and sharing

If the online AI assistant is enabled and you send a prompt, the app sends your prompt, recent chat history, and financial summaries (balance, income and expenses, spending totals by category, budgets, and recent transaction category, amount, and date) to the Finora backend. The backend forwards this request to Google Gemini to generate a response. Transaction names and notes are not included in the financial summary. Google and Firebase process data under their own terms and privacy practices.

How information is used

Information is used to provide account access, save and display your records, calculate summaries, and answer AI prompts you choose to send. We do not sell your financial records.

Retention and deletion

Signed-in records remain in Firebase until you delete your account or the records. Use Profile > Delete account to delete the Firebase account and its transaction, budget, goal, and profile records. A recent sign-in may be required to confirm deletion. You can also initiate deletion without the app by contacting the developer through the contact email shown on the Google Play listing. Guest data is temporary. Your device PIN is removed when you delete your account through the app.

Security

We use Firebase services and secure transport to handle account data. No online service or device storage can be guaranteed completely secure; keep your device and account credentials protected.

Children

Finora is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. For privacy questions, contact remphearomthunmony@gmail.com.
